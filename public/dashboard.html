Source & methods. Daily BTC/USD OHLCV candles from Bitstamp, via the public BTC daily price history repository. Daily return = close / prior close − 1. Realized volatility = sample standard deviation of the last 30 daily returns × √365. Drawdown = close / running maximum close − 1. The earliest BTC history is from a single exchange with thinner liquidity; repeated closes on low/no-trade days are retained as reported.
